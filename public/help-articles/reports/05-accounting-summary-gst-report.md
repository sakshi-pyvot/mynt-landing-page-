## What it is for

The Accounting Summary is Mynt’s GST report: sales, output GST, platform deductions, ads, TCS / TDS and net payout, outlet by outlet. Give it to your CA when you file. It is a working aid for your CA, not a GST return.

## Generate it

1. Open <b>Reports</b>. Under <b>Create or Schedule Report</b>, choose <b>Accounting Summary</b> as the <b>Report Type</b>.
2. Choose the <b>Platform</b> and <b>Outlets</b>. The <b>Report Period</b> starts on <b>Previous Month</b>, the usual filing period; change it if you need another.
3. Pick a <b>Delivery Channel</b> — Download, Email or WhatsApp — check the token cost, and generate.

![Create or Schedule Report with Accounting Summary, all outlets, Previous Month and Download selected](assets/accounting-summary-gst-report/step-01-form.png?v=20261118)
*Accounting Summary opens on Previous Month*

The report is a PDF. For the same figures as an Excel workbook, with outlet-wise formulas your CA can check, use [Download Raw Data](06-how-to-download-raw-data.md) and choose <b>Accounting Data</b>.

To get it every month, choose <b>Schedule Report</b> and <b>Monthly</b>. The period then follows the frequency, and the report is prepared after your platforms’ statements arrive.

## What’s in it

![The Accounting Summary PDF with its KPI tiles and GST Position](assets/accounting-summary-gst-report/step-02-report.png?v=20261118)
*Six totals, the waterfall to net payout, and the GST position*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Gross Sales** | Subtotal plus packaging, for delivered orders. |
| **GST Collected** | 5% on net sales, collected from customers. |
| **Paid by Platforms u/s 9(5)** | The part of that GST Zomato and Swiggy paid to the government for you. |
| **GST Payable by You** | What is left for you to deposit in GSTR-3B. |
| **GST on Fees & Ads** | 18% charged to you on commission, fees and ads. |
| **TCS + TDS Withheld** | Tax the platforms held back. These are credits to claim. |

Then a waterfall from gross sales to net payout, the <b>GST Position</b>, a split by platform, a table <b>By GSTIN</b> (returns are filed per GSTIN; outlets without one are grouped as <i>GSTIN not on file</i>), the outlet-by-outlet statement, and filing notes.

## Using it to file

- <b>GSTR-3B:</b> deposit the <b>GST Payable by You</b> amount. The monthly due date is the 20th (the 22nd for quarterly QRMP filers).
- <b>TCS u/s 52:</b> accept it on the GST portal after Zomato and Swiggy file GSTR-8. It then reaches your electronic cash ledger.
- <b>TDS u/s 194O:</b> match it with Form 26AS / AIS before you file income tax.

> **Tip:** The <b>Reconciled to platform payout</b> chip means the figures tie to what the platforms paid you. If it shows a <b>Residual</b> amount instead, the report says which outlet and platform to ask Mynt support to check.

## Common questions

**Why are cancelled orders not in sales?** — The report counts delivered orders by order date. Cancelled and rejected orders appear only under Payout for Cancelled.

**Why is “Net Payout after GST dues” lower than the bank payout?** — It takes off the GST you still have to deposit. It matches the Net Payout on your Mynt dashboard.

**Can I claim the GST on fees and ads?** — Only if your GSTIN is not on the 5% restaurant rate without input tax credit. Check with your CA.
