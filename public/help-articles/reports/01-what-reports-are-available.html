What Reports Are Available in Mynt

Reports · Read time: 3 minutes · Article only · Last updated 18 November 2026

One builder covers every report. You choose what it contains, which outlets and period it covers, and how it reaches you.

The report builder

Report TypeWhat the report contains.
PlatformOne or more platforms, or All Platforms.
OutletsWhich locations to include.
Report PeriodThe date range the report covers.
Delivery ChannelDownload, Email or WhatsApp. You can pick more than one.
Delivery TypeOne-time, or a recurring schedule.

The six reports

Owner's Business PulseMonthly executive summary for restaurant business performance
Sales & Demand IntelligenceDemand, sales mix, timing patterns, and outlet performance
Marketing & Promotion ROIAd efficiency, offer performance, and profitable sales lift
Payout and Settlement SummaryProfitability and payout-leakage breakdown for restaurant settlements
Operational Loss & RecoveryRefunds, cancellations, and operational issue prioritization
Accounting SummarySales, output GST, platform deductions, ads, TCS / TDS and net payout, outlet by outlet. See The Accounting Summary (GST report).

Owner's Business Pulse and Payout and Settlement Summary always cover whole months: pick a week and the form tells you, before you generate, which month the figures cover.

The page also offers Download Raw Data, so you can take the underlying order, ads and accounting figures into your own spreadsheet rather than only the summarised view. See How to download raw data.

Reports cost tokens

Each run spends tokens. Download is priced per file. Email and WhatsApp are priced per recipient, shown on the option as "N tokens/participant". The cost is shown before you generate anything — see generating a report and what consumes tokens.

One-time or recurring

  • One-Time — generate it now.
  • Schedule Report — have it delivered weekly, monthly or quarterly by email or WhatsApp. See scheduling reports.
