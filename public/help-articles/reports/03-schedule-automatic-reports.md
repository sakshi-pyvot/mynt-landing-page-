# How to Schedule Automatic Reports

*Reports · Read time: 3 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026*

The same builder can deliver a report on a recurring basis instead of once. Set it up in the same place.

## One-Time or Schedule

![The delivery type control offering One-Time or Schedule Report](assets/schedule-automatic-reports/step-01-delivery-type.png?v=20261022)
*Switch <strong>Delivery Type</strong> to <strong>Schedule Report</strong> to make it recurring*

1. Fill in the report as you would for a one-off: **Report Type**, **Platform** and **Outlets**.
2. Set **Delivery Type** to **Schedule Report**. **Download** disappears from the channels, because a schedule cannot be downloaded.
3. Choose the **Delivery Channel** &mdash; **Email**, **WhatsApp** or both &mdash; and type the recipients.
4. Choose the **Frequency**: **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Quarterly**. **Report Period** then locks to match, for example **Auto &mdash; Previous Week**.
5. Fill in **Start Date**. You do not pick a day or time: Mynt sends each report after the Wednesday payout statements that follow the period, from 8 pm IST. **When will I get my report?** on the form explains this.
6. Press **Schedule Report**. Mynt confirms with **Schedule created**.

## Tokens are spent per run

A schedule spends its token cost *every time it runs*, not once when you create it. A weekly schedule runs about four times a month, so it costs about four times what the same monthly one does. Check the cost summary before saving, and keep an eye on your balance &mdash; see [checking your balance](../tokens/03-how-to-check-token-usage-balance-get-more.md).

> **Tip:** Email and WhatsApp are priced per recipient, so every extra recipient adds to the cost of every run.

## Choosing a sensible frequency

| | |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Covers the previous week. Matches most payout cycles, so the data is usually complete. |
| **Monthly** | Covers the previous month. Accounting and reconciliation. |
| **Quarterly** | Covers the previous quarter. Quarterly reviews. |

Weekly generally lines up best with when platforms actually settle &mdash; see [how often Mynt syncs](../email-integration/05-how-often-sync.md).
