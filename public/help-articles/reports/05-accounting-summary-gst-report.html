The Accounting Summary (GST Report)

Output GST, the GST the platforms paid for you, TCS, TDS and net payout, outlet by outlet: the figures your CA needs to file.

Setting up last month’s Accounting Summary for every outlet and checking its token cost

What it is for

The Accounting Summary is Mynt’s GST report: sales, output GST, platform deductions, ads, TCS / TDS and net payout, outlet by outlet. Give it to your CA when you file. It is a working aid for your CA, not a GST return.

Generate it

  1. Open Reports. Under Create or Schedule Report, choose Accounting Summary as the Report Type.
  2. Choose the Platform and Outlets. The Report Period starts on Previous Month, the usual filing period; change it if you need another.
  3. Pick a Delivery Channel — Download, Email or WhatsApp — check the token cost, and generate.

The report is a PDF. For the same figures as an Excel workbook, with outlet-wise formulas your CA can check, use Download Raw Data and choose Accounting Data.

To get it every month, choose Schedule Report and Monthly. The period then follows the frequency, and the report is prepared after your platforms’ statements arrive.

What’s in it

Gross SalesSubtotal plus packaging, for delivered orders.
GST Collected5% on net sales, collected from customers.
Paid by Platforms u/s 9(5)The part of that GST Zomato and Swiggy paid to the government for you.
GST Payable by YouWhat is left for you to deposit in GSTR-3B.
GST on Fees & Ads18% charged to you on commission, fees and ads.
TCS + TDS WithheldTax the platforms held back. These are credits to claim.

Then a waterfall from gross sales to net payout, the GST Position, a split by platform, a table By GSTIN (returns are filed per GSTIN; outlets without one are grouped as GSTIN not on file), the outlet-by-outlet statement, and filing notes.

Using it to file

  • GSTR-3B: deposit the GST Payable by You amount. The monthly due date is the 20th (the 22nd for quarterly QRMP filers).
  • TCS u/s 52: accept it on the GST portal after Zomato and Swiggy file GSTR-8. It then reaches your electronic cash ledger.
  • TDS u/s 194O: match it with Form 26AS / AIS before you file income tax.
The Reconciled to platform payout chip means the figures tie to what the platforms paid you. If it shows a Residual amount instead, the report says which outlet and platform to ask Mynt support to check.

Common questions

Why are cancelled orders not in sales?
The report counts delivered orders by order date. Cancelled and rejected orders appear only under Payout for Cancelled.
Why is “Net Payout after GST dues” lower than the bank payout?
It takes off the GST you still have to deposit. It matches the Net Payout on your Mynt dashboard.
Can I claim the GST on fees and ads?
Only if your GSTIN is not on the 5% restaurant rate without input tax credit. Check with your CA.
