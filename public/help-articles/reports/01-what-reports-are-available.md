# What Reports Are Available in Mynt

*Reports · Read time: 3 minutes · Article only · Last updated 18 November 2026*

One builder covers every report. You choose what it contains, which outlets and period it covers, and how it reaches you.

---

## The report builder

![The Create or Schedule Report card with report type, platform, outlets and period](assets/what-reports-are-available/step-01-report-builder.png?v=20261022)
*Four choices define the report; two more define how it is delivered*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Report Type** | What the report contains. |
| **Platform** | One or more platforms, or **All Platforms**. |
| **Outlets** | Which locations to include. |
| **Report Period** | The date range the report covers. |
| **Delivery Channel** | **Download**, **Email** or **WhatsApp**. You can pick more than one. |
| **Delivery Type** | One-time, or a recurring schedule. |

## The six reports

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner's Business Pulse** | Monthly executive summary for restaurant business performance |
| **Sales & Demand Intelligence** | Demand, sales mix, timing patterns, and outlet performance |
| **Marketing & Promotion ROI** | Ad efficiency, offer performance, and profitable sales lift |
| **Payout and Settlement Summary** | Profitability and payout-leakage breakdown for restaurant settlements |
| **Operational Loss & Recovery** | Refunds, cancellations, and operational issue prioritization |
| **Accounting Summary** | Sales, output GST, platform deductions, ads, TCS / TDS and net payout, outlet by outlet. See [The Accounting Summary (GST report)](05-accounting-summary-gst-report.md). |

Owner's Business Pulse and Payout and Settlement Summary always cover whole months: pick a week and the form tells you, before you generate, which month the figures cover.

The page also offers **Download Raw Data**, so you can take the underlying order, ads and accounting figures into your own spreadsheet rather than only the summarised view. See [How to download raw data](06-how-to-download-raw-data.md).

## Reports cost tokens

Each run spends tokens. **Download** is priced per file. **Email** and **WhatsApp** are priced per recipient, shown on the option as "N tokens/participant". The cost is shown before you generate anything &mdash; see [generating a report](02-how-to-generate-and-download-report.md) and [what consumes tokens](../tokens/02-which-mynt-features-consume-tokens.md).

## One-time or recurring

- **One-Time** &mdash; generate it now.
- **Schedule Report** &mdash; have it delivered weekly, monthly or quarterly by email or WhatsApp. See [scheduling reports](03-schedule-automatic-reports.md).
