# How Often Does Mynt Sync New Email Data?

*Email Integration · Read time: 3 minutes · Article only · Last updated 25 August 2026*

Mynt checks continuously, but it can only find what the platforms have sent. That distinction explains almost every "why is it not updated yet" question.

---

## Two different clocks

| | |
|---|---|
| **How often Mynt reads your mailbox** | Continuously. A new payout email is normally processed within minutes of arriving. |
| **How often the platform sends one** | On its own payout cycle &mdash; commonly weekly, not daily and never per order. |

The second clock is the slow one, and Mynt does not control it. Same-day sales usually appear only once that cycle&rsquo;s settlement email is sent.

## Forcing a re-check for new outlets

If you have just started trading at a new location, Mynt may not have noticed its restaurant ID yet. **Rescan mailboxes** re-reads your connected inboxes looking for new IDs.

![The Rescan mailboxes control in Settings, Email Integration](assets/how-often-sync/step-01-rescan-mailboxes.png)
*Re-checks your mailboxes for restaurant IDs Mynt has not seen before*

1. Go to **Settings → Email Integration**.
2. Press **Rescan mailboxes**.
3. Open **Settings → Outlet Mapping** and map any newly found IDs.

> **Tip:** Rescan looks for *restaurant IDs*. It does not conjure payout emails that have not arrived, so it will not fill a missing week.

## After first connecting

The first read covers the payout emails already in the mailbox, so history appears in one go rather than trickling in. How far back it reaches depends on your plan and on how far back those emails still exist.
