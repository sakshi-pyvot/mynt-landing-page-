# How to Complete Your Business Profile

*Getting Started · Read time: 3 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Your profile is what appears on every invoice Mynt raises. Get it right before your first purchase &mdash; an issued invoice cannot be changed afterwards.

## Where it lives

Go to **Settings → Account**.

![The account details form with company name, GST number, billing state and address](assets/complete-business-profile/step-01-account-details.png?v=20261022)
*GST number first &mdash; company name, billing state and address follow from it*

## Fill it in this order

1. Enter your **GST Number** and press **Verify**.
2. A **GSTIN Verified** box shows the details on the GST register. Check them and press **Use these details**.
3. Mynt fills in your **Company Name**, **Billing State** and **Billing Address** from the register. If the register has no address, type your **Billing Address** yourself.
4. Add the **Account Owner Name** and **Phone Number**.
5. Press **Save Changes**. If you have typed a GST number, you must verify it (or clear it) before you can save.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Company Name** | Read-only once a GSTIN is verified. Change or clear the GST number to edit it. |
| **Billing State** | Also derived from the GSTIN. It determines how GST is applied to your invoices. |
| **Email ID** | Your login address. Read-only &mdash; contact support to change it. |
| **Billing Address** | Filled from the GST register when it has one. Printed on every invoice. |

> **Tip:** Enter the GSTIN *before* buying anything. A GSTIN cannot be added to an invoice that has already been issued.
