# Getting Started with Mynt: The Complete Setup Guide

*Getting Started · Read time: 5 minutes · Article only · Last updated 25 August 2026*

Five steps take you from a new account to a dashboard with your own numbers in it. Each step has its own guide.

---

## The five steps

1. [Create your account and sign in](02-how-to-create-your-mynt-account-and-sign-in.md).
2. [Complete your business profile](03-how-to-complete-your-business-profile.md) &mdash; GST number, billing state and address.
3. Buy an outlet licence for each location you want Mynt to track.
4. [Connect the mailbox](05-how-to-connect-your-data-sources-to-mynt.md) that receives your payout emails.
5. [Map each restaurant ID to an outlet](04-how-to-add-your-first-brand-and-outlet.md).

## Step 3 — one licence per outlet

A licence activates one outlet for twelve months and covers every platform that outlet trades on. Nothing is collected for an outlet without one, so this comes before mapping. See [how to purchase licences](../billing/02-how-to-purchase-mynt-licences.md) and [what a licence costs](../billing/01-understanding-mynt-pricing-annual-outlet-licences.md).

## How long it takes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Steps 1 to 3** | About ten minutes, if your GST number is to hand. |
| **Step 4** | A minute, plus the Google sign-in. |
| **Step 5** | A few minutes once Mynt has read your mail and found the restaurant IDs. |
| **First full dashboard** | As soon as a payout email covering a traded period has been processed. |

> **Tip:** The single most common setup mistake is connecting the wrong mailbox &mdash; the one you sign in with, rather than the one the platforms email. Check that first if nothing appears.

## When it is working

The dashboard shows a **LAST UPDATED** date and figures against every headline card, and **Settings → Outlet Mapping** shows **Unmapped: 0**. See [finding your way around](06-understanding-mynt-navigation-date-filters-platform-filters-views.md).
