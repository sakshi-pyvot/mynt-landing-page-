Getting Started with Mynt: The Complete Setup Guide

Getting Started · Read time: 5 minutes · Article only · Last updated 25 August 2026

Five steps take you from a new account to a dashboard with your own numbers in it. Each step has its own guide.

The five steps

  1. Create your account and sign in.
  2. Complete your business profile — GST number, billing state and address.
  3. Buy an outlet licence for each location you want Mynt to track.
  4. Connect the mailbox that receives your payout emails.
  5. Map each restaurant ID to an outlet.

Step 3 — one licence per outlet

A licence activates one outlet for twelve months and covers every platform that outlet trades on. Nothing is collected for an outlet without one, so this comes before mapping. See how to purchase licences and what a licence costs.

How long it takes

Steps 1 to 3About ten minutes, if your GST number is to hand.
Step 4A minute, plus the Google sign-in.
Step 5A few minutes once Mynt has read your mail and found the restaurant IDs.
First full dashboardAs soon as a payout email covering a traded period has been processed.
The single most common setup mistake is connecting the wrong mailbox — the one you sign in with, rather than the one the platforms email. Check that first if nothing appears.

When it is working

The dashboard shows a LAST UPDATED date and figures against every headline card, and Settings → Outlet Mapping shows Unmapped: 0. See finding your way around.
