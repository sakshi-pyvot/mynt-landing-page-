How to Complete Your Business Profile

Getting Started · Read time: 3 minutes · Article only · Last updated 25 August 2026

Your profile is what appears on every invoice Mynt raises. Get it right before your first purchase — an issued invoice cannot be changed afterwards.

Where it lives

Go to Settings → Account.

Fill it in this order

  1. Enter your GST Number and press Verify.
  2. Check the Company Name and Billing State that appear — both are taken from the verified GSTIN.
  3. Add your Billing Address.
  4. Add the Account Owner Name and Phone Number.
  5. Press Save Changes.
Company NameRead-only once a GSTIN is verified. Change or clear the GST number to edit it.
Billing StateAlso derived from the GSTIN. It determines how GST is applied to your invoices.
Email IDYour login address. Read-only — contact support to change it.
Billing AddressPrinted on every invoice.
Enter the GSTIN before buying anything. A GSTIN cannot be added to an invoice that has already been issued.
