# How to Check Your Token Balance and Get More

*Tokens · Read time: 3 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Your balance is on the Billing page, and so are the packs. Both are two clicks from anywhere in Mynt.

---

## Checking the balance

Open **Billing**. Your balance is the **Available tokens** figure in the summary at the top of the page.

![Available tokens shown in the billing summary](assets/how-to-check-token-usage-balance-get-more/step-01-token-balance.png?v=20261022)
*Your current balance, alongside your licence summary*

The report builder shows it too, next to the cost of the run you are about to make &mdash; see [generating a report](../reports/02-how-to-generate-and-download-report.md).

## Buying more

![The token packs with their prices, GST and per-token cost](assets/how-to-check-token-usage-balance-get-more/step-02-token-packs.png?v=20261022)
*Each pack shows its price including GST, the base price plus GST beneath it, and the cost per token*

1. Open **Billing** and find **Token Purchase**.
2. Compare the packs. Each shows its price including GST, the base price plus GST on a line beneath, and the cost per token. No GST is added at checkout.
3. Press **Buy Now** on the pack you want.
4. A dialog opens with the total. Press **Pay &#8377;&hellip; via Razorpay** and Razorpay opens in a new tab, where you choose how to pay. When the payment settles, Mynt shows **Payment Confirmed**, **Payment Pending** or **Payment Failed**. If you close the dialog while a payment is in progress, Mynt asks **Cancel this payment?**

The purchase appears in **Invoices & Payment History** with its own invoice &mdash; see [viewing and downloading invoices](../billing/05-how-to-view-download-invoices.md).

## Choosing a pack

| | |
|---|---|
| **Occasional use** | The smallest pack is enough for ad-hoc PIA questions and the odd report. |
| **Regular reporting** | A mid pack. Work out your monthly burn from your schedules first. |
| **Frequent schedules** | The largest pack &mdash; weekly runs to many recipients add up quickly. |

> **Tip:** Before topping up, check [your report schedules](../reports/04-how-to-manage-report-schedules.md). Turning off one unread weekly report often saves more than the next pack up.
