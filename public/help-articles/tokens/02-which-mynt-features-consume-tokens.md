# Which Mynt Features Consume Tokens?

*Tokens · Read time: 3 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Tokens are spent by PIA (AI Assistant) queries, reports and other exports, and notifications. Reports are usually the largest.

---

## Reports — the cost depends on channel and recipients

![Report delivery channels showing their individual token costs](assets/which-mynt-features-consume-tokens/step-01-report-token-costs.png?v=20261022)
*Each delivery option shows its price &mdash; Email and WhatsApp are priced per participant*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Download** | Charged once per file. |
| **Email** | Charged per participant &mdash; every To and CC address. |
| **WhatsApp** | Charged per participant &mdash; every WhatsApp number. |

The builder shows **Tokens / run** for the report as it stands, so the figure changes as you add or remove recipients. A *scheduled* report spends that cost every time it runs. A weekly schedule sent to many recipients is the fastest way to spend a balance &mdash; see [scheduling automatic reports](../reports/03-schedule-automatic-reports.md).

## PIA (AI Assistant)

Each answer is priced after it runs, from what it actually cost to produce. You can ask a question as long as your balance is above zero. An answer that costs more than what is left can take your balance below zero. Questions the assistant refuses are not charged.

## Notifications

Delivered alerts also consume tokens.

## What does not cost tokens

- Viewing dashboards and metric pages.
- Changing filters.
- Outlet mapping, brand and group changes.
- Reading invoices and licence information.

> **Tip:** If your balance falls without anyone using PIA, look for a forgotten report schedule before anything else &mdash; see [managing schedules](../reports/04-how-to-manage-report-schedules.md).
