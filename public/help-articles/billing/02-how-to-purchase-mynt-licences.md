# How to Purchase Mynt Licences

*Billing · Read time: 3 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Buying licences takes about a minute. Everything is priced on screen before you pay, and nothing is charged until you confirm.

## Step 1 — Open Billing

Choose **Billing** in the left menu. The **Purchase Outlet Licenses** card is on the right.

![The Purchase Outlet Licenses card with the quantity set to one](assets/how-to-purchase-mynt-licences/step-01-purchase-card.png?v=20261022)
*Each licence activates one outlet for 12 months from today*

## Step 2 — Choose how many outlets

Use **&minus;** and **+** under **How many outlets?**, or type the number. The card re-prices as you change it.

![The purchase card with three licences selected and the total updated](assets/how-to-purchase-mynt-licences/step-02-quantity-and-total.png?v=20261022)
*Three licences &mdash; price per licence, quantity, start, expiry and total, all before paying*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Price per license** | The annual per-outlet price, including GST. |
| **Licenses** | How many you are buying, as a multiplier. |
| **Start / Expiry** | Today, and twelve months from today. |
| **Total** | One-time, valid 12 months, including GST. This is the figure the payment button repeats. |

## Step 3 — Pay

The button restates the amount &mdash; **Purchase &mdash; Pay &#8377;&hellip;** &mdash; so you confirm a number, not a promise. A dialog opens with the total. Press **Pay &#8377;&hellip; via Razorpay** and Razorpay opens in a new tab, where you choose how to pay. When the payment settles, Mynt shows **Payment Confirmed**, **Payment Pending** or **Payment Failed**. If you close the dialog while a payment is in progress, Mynt asks **Cancel this payment?**

You can buy additional licences only once your base subscription is paid. Until then the card shows **Complete your base subscription before purchasing additional outlet licenses.**

> **Tip:** Buying more licences never disturbs the ones you already hold. Each new licence starts today and carries its own expiry; existing licences keep their original dates.

## Step 4 — Assign the licence to an outlet

A licence is bought first and assigned second. After payment, open **Billing** and press **View &amp; Edit Mapping** on the **Outlet Licenses** card. Press **Edit** on an **Unassigned** licence, choose the outlet, link its platform IDs and press **Save**. Saving links the licence to that outlet permanently: you cannot change the outlet or platform IDs later, only the outlet name. For finding your platform IDs, see [how to map platform outlets](../outlet-mapping/02-how-to-map-zomato-swiggy-platform-outlets.md).

## Common questions

**Can I buy for an outlet I have not added yet?** — Yes. Licences are counted, not named. Assign it when you add the outlet.

**Is the price shown with GST?** — Yes. The price per licence and the total include GST. Nothing is added at checkout.

**The payment failed &mdash; have I been charged?** — A failed payment shows as **Failed** in the payment history. It has no Retry link: start the purchase again. Only a **Pending** row has **Retry**. See the troubleshooting guide.
