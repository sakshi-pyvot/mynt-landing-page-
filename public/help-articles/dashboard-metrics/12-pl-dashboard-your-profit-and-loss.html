P&L Dashboard: Your Profit and Loss

Dashboard Metrics · Read time: 5 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 18 November 2026

Your online figures come in on their own. Add your own sales and costs, and Mynt builds a full profit-and-loss statement.

Reading the four totals, checking missing items, and adding a monthly bill

What the P&L shows

The P&L Dashboard is one profit-and-loss statement for your business: online, dine-in and every cost. Your Zomato, Swiggy and Toing figures come in on their own from your statements, up to the date shown at the top. Everything else, like dine-in sales, rent, salaries and bills, is what you add.

Total salesOnline sales after discounts and refunds, plus your own sales.
Gross profitTotal sales minus food and packing.
Operating profitGross profit minus monthly running costs: rent, salaries and bills.
Net profitOperating profit minus loan interest, depreciation and tax, plus other income.

First visit: set it up

The first time, Set up your P&L asks a few questions. Tick what else you sell (for example Dine-in or Takeaway) and add last month’s amount if you have it. Pick Your GST type: most restaurants are 5%, no input credit. Tick the costs you pay (Rent, Salaries and Electricity start ticked) and set a Food cost estimate %, used in months where you have not entered closing stock. Then Build my P&L. Anything you leave blank shows as Missing until you add it, and everything can be changed later.

Add an item

  1. Click Add item (or + Add item inside a section).
  2. Choose Money out or Money in, then a common item such as Rent or Gas cylinders, or Something else to type your own name.
  3. Under How does it come? pick Same every month (like rent or salaries) or Changes every month (like gas or sabzi bills). More ways adds Share of sales and Per order.
  4. Enter the amount. Turn on This total includes GST if the bill has GST in it; on the 5% or composition GST type the note tells you the GST can’t be claimed back, so the full amount counts as a cost.
  5. Choose who it is for: one outlet, several (with Different amount for each outlet), or All outlets, one bill. Then the sale type and the month.
  6. Check What will be saved, a plain sentence describing exactly what will be added, and click Save.
A bill counts in the month it is for, not the day you paid it. A monthly amount is spread over the days, so a week or a custom date range shows its share.

Loan or EMI and Big purchase have their own forms. Only a loan’s interest is a cost, not the part of the EMI that repays it; a big purchase is spread over the years you use it so one month does not look like a loss.

Missing items and “Not complete”

If a bill you add every month is not in yet, the banner says how many items are missing and the profit lines say Not complete. Click Fill missing items and, for each one, choose Same as last month, ₹0 for nothing this month, or Add the real amount.

When everything is in, you can Mark the month final. Later changes then ask before they touch that month; reopen it from Customise.

Change or remove an item

Click any row to open it. You can add a bill, change an amount Only this month or From this month onward, or Remove it. Removing asks how:

Stopped from next monthEarlier months keep their amounts. Nothing in the past changes.
Added by mistake, remove everywhereTaken out of every month. You can restore it from Customise for 90 days.

Rows that come from Zomato, Swiggy or Toing can’t be removed: they update on their own, and removing them would make your profit wrong.

Views and export

Switch the statement between Total, By outlet and By sale type, and turn on Show last period to add a comparison column. Export gives PDF, Excel, CSV or a WhatsApp image of the four totals. A PDF of a period with missing items is stamped DRAFT – not complete.

Saving

Changes save on their own, and Undo shows for a few seconds after each one. If a save fails, the page shows Not saved with Retry; your changes are kept.

If the P&L is changed in another tab or on another device while you have unsaved changes here, a banner says so and Update shows the latest version with your changes on top. Unsaved input in the Add item drawer is kept even if the page reloads: the drawer opens again with it.

Common questions

Why do the online figures stop on a certain date?
They come from your platform statements and run to the date shown at the top of the page.
Why can I only view the P&L?
Editing needs permission to manage the business’s items. Ask the account owner.
I typed “Zomato commission” and it asked why.
Commission and other platform figures are already counted automatically. Adding them again would count them twice, so Mynt asks for a reason first.
Where are TDS, TCS and loan repayments?
Under Not part of profit. They move cash but are not profit or loss.
