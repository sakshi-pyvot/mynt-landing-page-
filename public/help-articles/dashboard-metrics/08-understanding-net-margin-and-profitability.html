Understanding Net Margin and Profitability

Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 4 minutes · Article only · Last updated 25 August 2026

Net Margin is the one number that ties the rest together: what share of what customers paid you actually keep.

The card

The arithmetic is Net Payout ÷ Gross Order Value. Everything that reduces net payout — commission, gateway fees, delivery charges, discounts, ad spend, taxes — shows up here as a lower percentage.

What moves it

DiscountsReduce what you are paid without reducing gross order value. Margin falls.
Ad spendSame effect — paid out of your share.
Commission ratesFixed per outlet and platform; a shift in mix towards a higher-commission platform lowers margin.
Order mixMany small orders means gateway and packaging fees weigh more.
CancellationsCharges can still apply, so they cost margin.

Digging into when the money is made

The Earnings page breaks performance down by time — hourly, by mealtime and by day type — and adds insights that compare each window against a benchmark.

Comparing margin fairly

  1. Compare like periods — a partial week distorts everything.
  2. Compare per platform: the same business often runs several points apart on Zomato and Swiggy.
  3. Compare per outlet with VIEW BY → Outlet. One bad location can drag the group figure down.
  4. Check discounts and ad spend before concluding that commission is the problem.
Every percentage on the dashboard compares against the LAST PERIOD chip in the header — the period immediately before the one you are viewing. It is not a year-on-year comparison.
