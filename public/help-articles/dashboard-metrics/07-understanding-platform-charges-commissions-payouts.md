# Understanding Platform Charges, Commissions and Payouts

*Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 5 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026*

The gap between what customers paid and what reaches your bank. This is where it goes.

---

## Platform Charges

*Commission, payment gateway, long distance, government and other charges, net of any TDS 194H commission refund*

## Net Payout

![The Net Payout card on Overview](assets/understanding-platform-charges-commissions-payouts/step-02-net-payout-card.png?v=20261022)
*What actually reaches you &mdash; this is the figure to compare against your bank*

If you are reconciling against a bank statement, **Net Payout** is the number to use, not Gross Order Value. It is gross order value minus ad spend, discounts, platform charges and complaint refunds, plus any payout for cancelled orders.

## What makes up the charges

Choose **Charges** in the left menu for the breakdown. The table follows **View By**, so rows are outlets, brands or groups.

![The Outlet Charges table breaking charges down per outlet](assets/understanding-platform-charges-commissions-payouts/step-03-charges-page.png?v=20261022)
*Per outlet: gross order value, each charge type, the total, and <strong>Eff. %</strong> &mdash; Platform Charges as a share of gross order value*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Commission** | The platform&rsquo;s cut of each order &mdash; usually the largest single line. |
| **PG Charges** | Payment gateway charges for collecting the money. |
| **Long Distance Fee** | Delivery surcharges on orders beyond the standard radius. |
| **Government Charges** | Government charges: taxes and statutory deductions. |
| **Other Charges** | Remaining platform fees that are not commission, payment gateway, long distance or government charges. Packaging is not here: it is part of gross order value. |

The table can be sorted by **Highest Platform Charges %**, **Highest Commission** or **Highest Platform Charges**. Sorting by Platform Charges % (the **Eff. %** column) surfaces the outlets giving away the most per rupee of sales.

## Why two outlets pay different rates

- Commission rates are negotiated per outlet and per platform.
- Delivery surcharges depend on location and order distance.
- Order mix matters &mdash; more small orders means gateway fees weigh more heavily.

## Reconciling against your bank

1. Set the date range to the payout cycle, not the calendar month.
2. Compare against **Net Payout**.
3. Allow for cancellations and refunds settled in that cycle.
4. Still off? See [when dashboard numbers look wrong](../data-issues/03-what-to-do-when-dashboard-numbers-incorrect.md).
