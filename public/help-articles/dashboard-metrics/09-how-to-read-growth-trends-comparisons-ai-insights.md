# How to Read Growth Trends, Comparisons and PIA's Insights

*Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 5 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Below the headline cards sit the three tools for working out <em>why</em> a number moved: the platform split, the trend line, and the insights panel.

## Platform Comparison

![Platform comparison showing Zomato, Swiggy and Toing side by side](assets/how-to-read-growth-trends-comparisons-ai-insights/step-01-platform-comparison.png?v=20261022)
*The same metrics per platform, with each platform&rsquo;s share of gross order value*

Each platform card repeats the headline metrics &mdash; gross order value, orders, ads, Platform Charges, discounts, refunds, Net Payout and Payout % &mdash; so you can see which platform moved the group figure. Toing appears only if your account has Toing data. A share bar at the top shows the split at a glance.

> **Tip:** Payout % often differs by several points between platforms on the same menu. If the group Payout % fell, check whether the mix shifted rather than assuming rates changed.

## Trends

![The Trends chart with Orders as bars and Sales and Payout as lines over the selected period](assets/how-to-read-growth-trends-comparisons-ai-insights/step-02-trends.png?v=20261022)
*Orders, Sales and Payout on one chart &mdash; daily, hourly, by mealtime or by day type*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Orders / Sales / Payout** | All three on one chart: Orders as bars, Sales and Payout as lines. Click a name in the legend above the chart to hide or show it. |
| **Daily** | Day by day across the period &mdash; best for spotting a single bad day. |
| **Hourly** | By hour of day &mdash; best for staffing and kitchen capacity. |
| **Mealtime** | Grouped into meal windows. |
| **Day Type** | Weekday against weekend. |

A card tells you *that* something moved; the trend tells you *when*. A week that is down because of one closed day reads very differently from one that is down every day.

## PIA's Insights

![The PIA's Insights panel with prioritised observations](assets/how-to-read-growth-trends-comparisons-ai-insights/step-03-ai-insights.png?v=20261022)
*Each insight is tagged by priority. The chips at the top filter the list and show how many of each there are.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Act now** | The most urgent: something needs attention now. |
| **Review** | Worth a look, not urgent. |
| **Opportunity** | A positive movement worth building on. |

The chips beside the title &mdash; **All**, **Act now**, **Review** and **Opportunity** &mdash; each show a count. Pick one to see only that priority; **All** shows everything. A priority with no insights has no chip.

Insights are generated from the same filtered data as the cards, so changing the filters changes the insights. They point at where to look; the trend and comparison views tell you what actually happened.

## A workable order to read them in

1. Read the headline card that moved.
2. Check **Platform Comparison** &mdash; is it one platform or both?
3. Check **Trends** &mdash; is it one day or the whole period?
4. Read **PIA's Insights** for what the data itself flags.
5. Narrow **Select Outlet** to one outlet at a time to find the specific location.
