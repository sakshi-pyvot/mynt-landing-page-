# Understanding Net Payout and Payout %

*Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 4 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Payout % is the one number that ties the rest together: what share of what customers paid reaches you as Net Payout.

---

## The card

![The Payout % card on Overview](assets/understanding-net-margin-and-profitability/step-01-net-margin-card.png?v=20261022)
*Net Payout as a percentage of gross order value*

The arithmetic is **Net Payout &divide; Gross Order Value**. Net Payout is the rupee figure; Payout % is the same figure as a share of sales. Everything that reduces Net Payout &mdash; commission, gateway fees, delivery charges, discounts, ad spend, taxes &mdash; shows up here as a lower percentage.

Payout % is a payout ratio, not your final profit. Food, packaging, rent and staff costs never pass through the platforms, so they are not in it.

With no sales in the period there is nothing to take a percentage of. If Net Payout is still below zero &mdash; a charge for a cancelled order, say &mdash; Payout % reads **n/a**, and the info button beside it shows that Net Payout. With no sales and nothing below zero, it reads &mdash;.

## What moves it

| | |
|---|---|
| **Discounts** | Reduce what you are paid without reducing gross order value. Payout % falls. |
| **Ad spend** | Same effect &mdash; paid out of your share. |
| **Commission rates** | Fixed per outlet and platform; a shift in mix towards a higher-commission platform lowers Payout %. |
| **Order mix** | Many small orders means gateway fees weigh more. |
| **Cancellations** | Cancelled orders are not in gross order value, but any payout for them is added to Net Payout, so they can move Payout % either way. |

## Comparing Payout % fairly

1. Compare like periods &mdash; a partial week distorts everything.
2. Compare per platform: the same business often runs several points apart on Zomato and Swiggy.
3. Compare per outlet by choosing one outlet at a time in **Select Outlet**. One bad location can drag the group figure down.
4. Check discounts and ad spend before concluding that commission is the problem.

> **Tip:** Every percentage on the dashboard compares against the **Last Period** chip in the header &mdash; the period immediately before the one you are viewing. It is not a year-on-year comparison.
