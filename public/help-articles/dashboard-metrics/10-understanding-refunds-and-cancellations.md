# Understanding Refunds and Cancellations

*Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 4 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Not every order ends as a clean sale. Some are delivered and then refunded; others are cancelled and never reach the customer. The Refunds page shows both, as Zomato, Swiggy and Toing settle them with you.

---

## Refunds and cancellations are different things

| | |
|---|---|
| **Refund** | A delivered order where the platform deducted money from you &mdash; for a customer complaint, a refund, compensation or a recoupment. |
| **Cancellation** | An order that was not delivered. It is not part of gross order value, but the platform may still pay you something for it. |

This page is about the platforms&rsquo; refunds to your customers. It is not about Mynt&rsquo;s own refund policy.

Mynt only sees what the platforms put in their settlement emails. If a cancellation came with no payout, it never reaches Mynt, so it is not counted.

## The cards

Choose **Refunds** in the left menu. The two rows of cards at the top follow the period and filters you have selected.

![The Refunds dashboard's eight headline cards: Refund Amount, Refund value % of sales, Refunded Orders, Gross Order Value, Cancelled Orders, Cancelled payout % of sales, Payout for Cancelled Orders and Total Orders](assets/understanding-refunds-and-cancellations/step-01-refund-cards.png?v=20261022)
*Refunds on the top row, cancellations on the bottom row*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Refund Amount** | The rupee value deducted for complaints, refunds, compensation and recoupment. |
| **Refund value % of sales** | Refund Amount as a share of gross order value. It measures value, not how many orders. |
| **Refunded Orders** | How many delivered orders had a refund or complaint deduction. |
| **Gross Order Value** | Sales on delivered orders, shown for reference. |
| **Cancelled Orders** | How many cancelled orders the platforms reported. |
| **Cancelled payout % of sales** | Payout for Cancelled Orders as a share of gross order value. It measures value, not the share of orders that were cancelled. |
| **Payout for Cancelled Orders** | What the platforms still paid you for cancelled orders. It is added to Net Payout. |
| **Total Orders** | Delivered orders in the period. |

> **Tip:** **Cancelled payout % of sales** is not a cancellation rate. It goes up when you are paid more for cancelled orders. To see how many orders were cancelled, use **Cancelled orders %** on the platform cards or in the outlet table.

## Refund & Cancellation Trends

The chart plots one series at a time. Pick **Refund Amt**, **Refund value % of sales**, **Refunded Orders**, **Cancelled Orders** or **Cancelled order value % of sales**. The two percentages are values as a share of gross order value, and they are drawn as bars. **Cancelled order value % of sales** is the value of cancelled orders &divide; gross order value. It is not the same figure as **Cancelled payout % of sales**, which uses what you were paid for those orders.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | Follows the date range you have selected. |
| **Weekly** | Always the latest 4 weeks of data, whatever date range you have selected. |
| **Monthly** | Always the latest 4 months of data, whatever date range you have selected. |

## Platform Refund Split

There is one card per platform. Zomato and Swiggy are always there; Toing appears only if your account has Toing data. The cards show every platform even when the platform filter is set, so you can always compare them side by side.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Refund value % of sales** | Refund value as a share of that platform&rsquo;s gross order value. |
| **refunded of** | Refunded orders out of that platform&rsquo;s delivered orders. |
| **Cancelled orders %** | Cancelled orders as a share of that platform&rsquo;s delivered orders. This one counts orders, not value. |

Both rates are usually under 1%, so the bars treat 1% as full. Read the number next to each bar for the exact figure.

## Outlet Refunds

Each outlet gets one row. Sort by **Highest Refund Amount** or **Highest Cancelled orders %**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Refund Amount** | The rupee value of refunds at that outlet. |
| **Refund value % of sales** | Refund value as a share of the outlet&rsquo;s gross order value. |
| **Cancelled orders %** | Cancelled orders as a share of the outlet&rsquo;s delivered orders. |

The colour on **Cancelled orders %** ranks your outlets against each other. The top third show red, the middle third amber and the bottom third green.

## Policies and insights

The **Cancellation & refund policy** button, top right, opens the Zomato and Swiggy cancellation and refund policies. **PIA's Insights**, bottom right, points out what moved in the period.

> **Tip:** Every percentage on the dashboard compares against the **Last Period** chip in the header &mdash; the period immediately before the one you are viewing. It is not a year-on-year comparison.

## Common questions

**Why does Cancelled Orders not match the platform app?** — Mynt counts the cancellations the platforms report in their settlement emails. A cancellation with no payout is not in those emails.

**Why did a refund show up in an older period?** — A later settlement email can add a refund or recoupment to a period you have already looked at.

**Is a high Cancelled payout % of sales bad?** — Not on its own. It means you were paid more for cancelled orders. To see how many orders were cancelled, look at **Cancelled Orders** and **Cancelled orders %**.
