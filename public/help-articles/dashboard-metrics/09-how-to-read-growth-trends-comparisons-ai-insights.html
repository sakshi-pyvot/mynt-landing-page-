How to Read Growth Trends, Comparisons and PIA's Insights

Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 5 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026

Below the headline cards sit the three tools for working out why a number moved: the platform split, the trend line, and the insights panel.

Watch it done

Platform Comparison, every Trends view, and opening PIA’s Insights

Platform Comparison

Each platform card repeats the headline metrics — gross order value, orders, ads, Platform Charges, discounts, refunds, Net Payout and Payout % — so you can see which platform moved the group figure. Toing appears only if your account has Toing data. A share bar at the top shows the split at a glance.

Payout % often differs by several points between platforms on the same menu. If the group Payout % fell, check whether the mix shifted rather than assuming rates changed.

Trends

Orders / Sales / PayoutAll three on one chart: Orders as bars, Sales and Payout as lines. Click a name in the legend above the chart to hide or show it.
DailyDay by day across the period — best for spotting a single bad day.
HourlyBy hour of day — best for staffing and kitchen capacity.
MealtimeGrouped into meal windows.
Day TypeWeekday against weekend.

A card tells you that something moved; the trend tells you when. A week that is down because of one closed day reads very differently from one that is down every day.

PIA's Insights

Act nowThe most urgent: something needs attention now.
ReviewWorth a look, not urgent.
OpportunityA positive movement worth building on.

The chips beside the title — All, Act now, Review and Opportunity — each show a count. Pick one to see only that priority; All shows everything. A priority with no insights has no chip.

Insights are generated from the same filtered data as the cards, so changing the filters changes the insights. They point at where to look; the trend and comparison views tell you what actually happened.

A workable order to read them in

  1. Read the headline card that moved.
  2. Check Platform Comparison — is it one platform or both?
  3. Check Trends — is it one day or the whole period?
  4. Read PIA's Insights for what the data itself flags.
  5. Narrow Select Outlet to one outlet at a time to find the specific location.
