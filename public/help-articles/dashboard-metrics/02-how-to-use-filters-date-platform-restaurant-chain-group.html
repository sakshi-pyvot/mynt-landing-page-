How to Use the Filters: Date, Platform, Outlet, Brand and Group

Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 4 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026

The filter panel decides what every number on the page counts. Four controls, and they apply everywhere.

Watch it done

Changing the period, the platform and the outlet selection, and applying them

The panel

Date

WeekThe 7 days ending on the latest date Mynt holds data for.
MonthFrom the 1st of the latest month up to the latest date with data.
Prev. monthThe full calendar month before the latest month.
The date rangeThe field under the three options. Click it to open a calendar and pick a start and an end date — any range of up to 90 days, between your first and latest data dates. Use this to chase a specific period.

Whatever you pick, the date-range field and the header chip show the exact dates. Read those rather than trusting the label.

Platform

All Platforms is the default. Open the list and tick one or more platforms to narrow it. Zomato and Swiggy are always listed; Toing appears only if your outlets have Toing data. Selecting one platform is the quickest way to tell whether a problem is platform-specific — see why one platform can be missing.

View By

OutletEach licensed location on its own.
BrandOutlets grouped by the brand they trade under.
GroupOutlets grouped by the operational groups you defined.

This changes how things are grouped, not what is included. On Overview it changes what the selector below lists. On pages with tables, such as Topline, Advertisements and Charges, it also groups the table rows. See brands and groups.

Select Outlet

The label follows View By: Select Outlet, Select Brand or Select Group. Search, tick the ones you want, or use Select all and Clear. A part-selected outlet list is the most common reason a total looks too low.

Apply and Reset

  1. Set the controls you want.
  2. Press Apply. The page reloads its figures.
  3. Press Reset to go straight back to the default: Week, all platforms, all outlets and the first View By option. Reset takes effect at once, without Apply.
When a number looks wrong, press Reset first and read it again. It rules out the most likely cause in one click.
