## What the Briefing is

Briefing is the first page you see when you sign in. It covers your whole business on one screen: where every ₹100 your customers ordered went, what changed against the period before, and what to look at next. Mynt works out the figures from your statements; Pia, the Mynt assistant, writes the words around them, and every number Pia writes is checked against the figures.

There is no filter panel here. A briefing always covers all your outlets and platforms.

## Pick a period

![The Latest week, This month and Last month tabs with the Data up to chip](assets/your-briefing/step-01-periods.png?v=20261118)
*Three periods, and the date your statements run to*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Latest week** | The last full week, Monday to Sunday. The page opens on this one. |
| **This month** | The calendar month so far. |
| **Last month** | The whole previous month. |

Under the tabs, <b>Data up to</b> shows the last day your statements cover, and the second chip says what the period is compared with. If some statements for the month have not arrived yet, the page says so: <i>“Some statements for this month haven’t arrived yet, so it is not complete.”</i>

## Where every ₹100 went

![The ₹100 note drawing beside the payout breakdown](assets/your-briefing/step-02-rupee-note.png?v=20261118)
*Each coloured band is a share of every ₹100 ordered; hover a band to light up its row*

The headline reads like <i>“Of every ₹100 your customers ordered … ₹52.72 is estimated to come back to you.”</i> Beside the note drawing, each row shows its share of ₹100 and the rupee amount:

- <b>Estimated payout</b>, and under it <b>incl. cancelled-order payout</b> when there is any
- <b>Platform charges</b>: Commission, Payment gateway, Government charges and, when there are any, Other platform fees
- <b>Discounts you paid</b>
- <b>Ad spend (incl. GST)</b>
- <b>Refunds for complaints</b>

Below it, one line adds it all up: what was ordered, minus charges, discounts, ads and refunds, plus cancelled-order payout, equals the estimated payout.

> **Tip:** <b>Estimated payout is not money received, and not profit.</b> Food, rent and staff costs are not in this figure. For profit, use the [P&L Dashboard](12-pl-dashboard-your-profit-and-loss.md). Totals can differ by ₹1 because parts are rounded.

## Compared with the period before

The headline ends with the change: <i>“down from ₹49.92 per ₹100 in …”</i>. When one platform’s statements stop earlier than another’s, or the months have different lengths, Mynt compares the same days on both sides and words it as <i>“₹0.72 less of every ₹100 than …”</i>, so you are never comparing a short period with a full one.

## The period in full, and what stands out

![The What stands out panel with Going well and Needs a look points](assets/your-briefing/step-03-stands-out.png?v=20261118)
*Points are tagged Going well or Needs a look*

<b>The period in full</b> is Pia’s summary. Hover a sentence about charges, discounts or ads and its band lights up on the note. <b>What stands out</b> lists the points worth your attention, each tagged <b>Going well</b> or <b>Needs a look</b>.

If the summary is missing but the figures are there, the figures are still complete. Pia either could not write it at that moment, or its numbers did not pass the check, in which case it is not shown at all.

## What to do next

![Numbered next steps with Open Advertisements and Open Discounts links](assets/your-briefing/step-04-next-steps.png?v=20261118)
*Each link opens a Mynt page already filtered to the briefing’s period*

Each step has a title, the reason, and a link such as <b>Open Advertisements</b> or <b>Open Discounts</b>. Clicking it sets your dashboard filters to the briefing’s dates, and to the platform and outlet the step is about, then opens that page. Your filters stay set that way afterwards; change them from the filter panel as usual.

## Your kitchens

With two or more outlets, <b>Your kitchens</b> shows one card per outlet: what it kept of its own ₹100, its sales and orders, a short note, and an <b>Open Topline</b> link for that outlet. With one outlet this section is not shown, because it would repeat the note above.

## Tokens

Pia’s writing uses tokens, like a chat answer. After a briefing is made the page shows <b>Used N tokens</b>. Opening the same period again, reloading, or switching back to a tab you already opened does not charge again. A briefing that comes back without Pia’s summary is not charged.

## Common questions

**It says I’m out of tokens.** — <i>“You’re out of tokens, so the briefing wasn’t made.”</i> Buy tokens from the link and open the page again. See [checking your balance and getting more](../tokens/03-how-to-check-token-usage-balance-get-more.md).

**It asks me to turn on Mynt Assistant.** — Pia writes the briefing, so the AI processing agreement has to be accepted first. Click <b>Open PIA</b>, agree, and come back.

**This month says no statements have arrived.** — Early in the month there is nothing to read yet. <b>Latest week</b> and <b>Last month</b> have your recent figures.

**Why can’t I see Briefing?** — It needs access to Pia. Without it, Mynt opens on the Overview instead.

**It says I’ve reached today’s limit.** — Pia has a daily limit. It resets at midnight.
