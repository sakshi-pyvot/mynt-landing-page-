Understanding Refunds and Cancellations

Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 4 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026

Not every order ends as a clean sale. Some are delivered and then refunded; others are cancelled and never reach the customer. The Refunds page shows both, as Zomato, Swiggy and Toing settle them with you.

Refunds and cancellations are different things

RefundA delivered order where the platform deducted money from you — for a customer complaint, a refund, compensation or a recoupment.
CancellationAn order that was not delivered. It is not part of gross order value, but the platform may still pay you something for it.

This page is about the platforms’ refunds to your customers. It is not about Mynt’s own refund policy.

Mynt only sees what the platforms put in their settlement emails. If a cancellation came with no payout, it never reaches Mynt, so it is not counted.

The cards

Choose Refunds in the left menu. The two rows of cards at the top follow the period and filters you have selected.

Refund AmountThe rupee value deducted for complaints, refunds, compensation and recoupment.
Refund value % of salesRefund Amount as a share of gross order value. It measures value, not how many orders.
Refunded OrdersHow many delivered orders had a refund or complaint deduction.
Gross Order ValueSales on delivered orders, shown for reference.
Cancelled OrdersHow many cancelled orders the platforms reported.
Cancelled payout % of salesPayout for Cancelled Orders as a share of gross order value. It measures value, not the share of orders that were cancelled.
Payout for Cancelled OrdersWhat the platforms still paid you for cancelled orders. It is added to Net Payout.
Total OrdersDelivered orders in the period.
Cancelled payout % of sales is not a cancellation rate. It goes up when you are paid more for cancelled orders. To see how many orders were cancelled, use Cancelled orders % on the platform cards or in the outlet table.

Refund & Cancellation Trends

The chart plots one series at a time. Pick Refund Amt, Refund value % of sales, Refunded Orders, Cancelled Orders or Cancelled order value % of sales. The two percentages are values as a share of gross order value, and they are drawn as bars. Cancelled order value % of sales is the value of cancelled orders ÷ gross order value. It is not the same figure as Cancelled payout % of sales, which uses what you were paid for those orders.

DailyFollows the date range you have selected.
WeeklyAlways the latest 4 weeks of data, whatever date range you have selected.
MonthlyAlways the latest 4 months of data, whatever date range you have selected.

Platform Refund Split

There is one card per platform. Zomato and Swiggy are always there; Toing appears only if your account has Toing data. The cards show every platform even when the platform filter is set, so you can always compare them side by side.

Refund value % of salesRefund value as a share of that platform’s gross order value.
refunded ofRefunded orders out of that platform’s delivered orders.
Cancelled orders %Cancelled orders as a share of that platform’s delivered orders. This one counts orders, not value.

Both rates are usually under 1%, so the bars treat 1% as full. Read the number next to each bar for the exact figure.

Outlet Refunds

Each outlet gets one row. Sort by Highest Refund Amount or Highest Cancelled orders %.

Refund AmountThe rupee value of refunds at that outlet.
Refund value % of salesRefund value as a share of the outlet’s gross order value.
Cancelled orders %Cancelled orders as a share of the outlet’s delivered orders.

The colour on Cancelled orders % ranks your outlets against each other. The top third show red, the middle third amber and the bottom third green.

Policies and insights

The Cancellation & refund policy button, top right, opens the Zomato and Swiggy cancellation and refund policies. PIA’s Insights, bottom right, points out what moved in the period.

Every percentage on the dashboard compares against the Last Period chip in the header — the period immediately before the one you are viewing. It is not a year-on-year comparison.

Common questions

Why does Cancelled Orders not match the platform app?
Mynt counts the cancellations the platforms report in their settlement emails. A cancellation with no payout is not in those emails.
Why did a refund show up in an older period?
A later settlement email can add a refund or recoupment to a period you have already looked at.
Is a high Cancelled payout % of sales bad?
Not on its own. It means you were paid more for cancelled orders. To see how many orders were cancelled, look at Cancelled Orders and Cancelled orders %.
