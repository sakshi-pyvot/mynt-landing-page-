# Understanding the Mynt Dashboard

*Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 5 minutes · Article only · Last updated 22 October 2026*

Overview answers one question: how did the business do over a chosen period? Everything on it is either the period you picked, a number for that period, or a comparison against the period before.

---

## Read the header first

![The Overview header with last updated, the selected period and the comparison period](assets/understanding-the-mynt-dashboard/step-01-header-and-period.png?v=20261022)
*Three chips: how fresh the data is, what you are looking at, and what it is measured against*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Last updated** | The most recent day Mynt holds data for. If it is stale, no newer payout email has arrived. |
| **The period chip** | The range currently on screen &mdash; the label plus its exact dates. |
| **Last Period** | The range every *vs last period* figure is compared against. |

## The headline cards

Ten cards summarise the period. They fall into three groups: what customers spent, what it cost you, and what you kept.

![The ten headline metric cards on Overview](assets/understanding-the-mynt-dashboard/step-02-headline-cards.png?v=20261022)
*Sales at the top, costs in the middle, what you keep at the end*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Gross Order Value** | What customers were billed, before any platform deduction. |
| **Total Orders** | Orders in the period. |
| **Average Order Value** | Gross order value divided by orders. |
| **Net Payout** | What is payable to you: gross order value minus ad spend, discounts, platform charges and complaint refunds, plus any payout for cancelled orders. |
| **Ad Spend (incl. GST)** | What you spent on platform advertising. |
| **Platform Charges** | Commission, payment gateway, long distance, government and other platform charges, net of any TDS 194H commission refund. |
| **Discounts** | The value of discounts and coupons applied. |
| **Refund for Complaint** | Refunds issued against customer complaints. |
| **Payout for Cancelled Orders** | What you were still paid on cancelled orders. It is added to Net Payout. |
| **Payout %** | Net Payout as a percentage of gross order value. A payout ratio, not your final profit. |

> **Tip:** Every percentage on the dashboard compares against the **Last Period** chip in the header &mdash; the period immediately before the one you are viewing. It is not a year-on-year comparison.

## Below the cards

- **Platform Comparison** &mdash; the same metrics split by Zomato and Swiggy, plus Toing if your account has Toing data.
- **Trends** &mdash; one chart with Orders as bars and Sales and Payout as lines, in **Daily**, **Hourly**, **Mealtime** or **Day Type** view.
- **PIA's Insights** &mdash; short prompts about what moved and what to look at.

See [reading trends, comparisons and PIA's Insights](09-how-to-read-growth-trends-comparisons-ai-insights.md).

## Everything is filtered

Every number obeys the filter panel on the left. If a figure looks wrong, check the filters before anything else &mdash; see [using the filters](02-how-to-use-filters-date-platform-restaurant-chain-group.md).
