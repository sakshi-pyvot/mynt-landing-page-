# How to Use the Filters: Date, Platform, Outlet, Brand and Group

*Dashboard & Metrics · Read time: 4 minutes · Includes a short video · Last updated 22 October 2026*

The filter panel decides what every number on the page counts. Four controls, and they apply everywhere.

## Date

| | |
|---|---|
| **Week** | The 7 days ending on the latest date Mynt holds data for. |
| **Month** | From the 1st of the latest month up to the latest date with data. |
| **Prev. month** | The full calendar month before the latest month. |
| **The date range** | The field under the three options. Click it to open a calendar and pick a start and an end date &mdash; any range of up to 90 days, between your first and latest data dates. Use this to chase a specific period. |

Whatever you pick, the date-range field and the header chip show the exact dates. Read those rather than trusting the label.

## Platform

**All Platforms** is the default. Open the list and tick one or more platforms to narrow it. **Zomato** and **Swiggy** are always listed; **Toing** appears only if your outlets have Toing data. Selecting one platform is the quickest way to tell whether a problem is platform-specific &mdash; see [why one platform can be missing](../data-issues/04-why-zomato-swiggy-data-missing-incomplete.md).

## View By

| | |
|---|---|
| **Outlet** | Each licensed location on its own. |
| **Brand** | Outlets grouped by the brand they trade under. |
| **Group** | Outlets grouped by the operational groups you defined. |

This changes how things are grouped, not what is included. On Overview it changes what the selector below lists. On pages with tables, such as **Topline**, **Advertisements** and **Charges**, it also groups the table rows. See [brands and groups](../outlet-mapping/04-how-outlet-mapping-works-multiple-outlets-chains-groups.md).

## Select Outlet

The label follows **View By**: **Select Outlet**, **Select Brand** or **Select Group**. Search, tick the ones you want, or use **Select all** and **Clear**. A part-selected outlet list is the most common reason a total looks too low.

## Apply and Reset

1. Set the controls you want.
2. Press **Apply**. The page reloads its figures.
3. Press **Reset** to go straight back to the default: **Week**, all platforms, all outlets and the first **View By** option. Reset takes effect at once, without **Apply**.

> **Tip:** When a number looks wrong, press **Reset** first and read it again. It rules out the most likely cause in one click.
