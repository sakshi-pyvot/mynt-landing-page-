## What the P&L shows

The P&L Dashboard is one profit-and-loss statement for your business: online, dine-in and every cost. Your Zomato, Swiggy and Toing figures come in on their own from your statements, up to the date shown at the top. Everything else, like dine-in sales, rent, salaries and bills, is what you add.

![The P&L Dashboard with its four totals and the statement](assets/pl-dashboard-your-profit-and-loss/step-01-overview.png?v=20261118)
*Total sales, gross profit, operating profit and net profit, then the statement below*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Total sales** | Online sales after discounts and refunds, plus your own sales. |
| **Gross profit** | Total sales minus food and packing. |
| **Operating profit** | Gross profit minus monthly running costs: rent, salaries and bills. |
| **Net profit** | Operating profit minus loan interest, depreciation and tax, plus other income. |

## First visit: set it up

The first time, <b>Set up your P&L</b> asks a few questions. Tick what else you sell (for example Dine-in or Takeaway) and add last month’s amount if you have it. Pick <b>Your GST type</b>: most restaurants are <b>5%, no input credit</b>. Tick the costs you pay (Rent, Salaries and Electricity start ticked) and set a <b>Food cost estimate %</b>, used in months where you have not entered closing stock. Then <b>Build my P&L</b>. Anything you leave blank shows as Missing until you add it, and everything can be changed later.

## Add an item

![The Add an item drawer: Money out or Money in, a search box, Something else, and the items already in your P&L](assets/pl-dashboard-your-profit-and-loss/step-02-add-item.png?v=20261118)
*Pick an item, search for one, or choose Something else to type your own*

1. Click <b>Add item</b> (or <b>+ Add item</b> inside a section).
2. Choose <b>Money out</b> or <b>Money in</b>, then a common item such as Rent or Gas cylinders, or <b>Something else</b> to type your own name.
3. Under <b>How does it come?</b> pick <b>Same every month</b> (like rent or salaries) or <b>Changes every month</b> (like gas or sabzi bills). <b>More ways</b> adds <b>Share of sales</b> and <b>Per order</b>.
4. Enter the amount. Turn on <b>This total includes GST</b> if the bill has GST in it; on the 5% or composition GST type the note tells you the GST can’t be claimed back, so the full amount counts as a cost.
5. Choose who it is for: one outlet, several (with <b>Different amount for each outlet</b>), or <b>All outlets, one bill</b>. Then the sale type and the month.
6. Check <b>What will be saved</b>, a plain sentence describing exactly what will be added, and click <b>Save</b>.

> **Tip:** A bill counts in the month it is for, not the day you paid it. A monthly amount is spread over the days, so a week or a custom date range shows its share.

<b>Loan or EMI</b> and <b>Big purchase</b> have their own forms. Only a loan’s interest is a cost, not the part of the EMI that repays it; a big purchase is spread over the years you use it so one month does not look like a loss.

## Missing items and “Not complete”

![The Missing for November panel, listing each missing item with ₹0 and Add](assets/pl-dashboard-your-profit-and-loss/step-03-missing.png?v=20261118)
*Fill each missing item in one click; Same as last month appears when last month has an amount*

If a bill you add every month is not in yet, the banner says how many items are missing and the profit lines say <b>Not complete</b>. Click <b>Fill missing items</b> and, for each one, choose <b>Same as</b> last month, <b>₹0</b> for nothing this month, or <b>Add</b> the real amount.

When everything is in, you can <b>Mark</b> the month <b>final</b>. Later changes then ask before they touch that month; reopen it from <b>Customise</b>.

## Change or remove an item

Click any row to open it. You can add a bill, change an amount <b>Only this month</b> or <b>From this month onward</b>, or <b>Remove</b> it. Removing asks how:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stopped from next month** | Earlier months keep their amounts. Nothing in the past changes. |
| **Added by mistake, remove everywhere** | Taken out of every month. You can restore it from Customise for 90 days. |

Rows that come from Zomato, Swiggy or Toing can’t be removed: they update on their own, and removing them would make your profit wrong.

## Views and export

Switch the statement between <b>Total</b>, <b>By outlet</b> and <b>By sale type</b>, and turn on <b>Show last period</b> to add a comparison column. <b>Export</b> gives PDF, Excel, CSV or a WhatsApp image of the four totals. A PDF of a period with missing items is stamped <i>DRAFT – not complete</i>.

## Saving

Changes save on their own, and <b>Undo</b> shows for a few seconds after each one. If a save fails, the page shows <b>Not saved</b> with <b>Retry</b>; your changes are kept.

If the P&L is changed in another tab or on another device while you have unsaved changes here, a banner says so and <b>Update</b> shows the latest version with your changes on top. Unsaved input in the Add item drawer is kept even if the page reloads: the drawer opens again with it.

## Common questions

**Why do the online figures stop on a certain date?** — They come from your platform statements and run to the date shown at the top of the page.

**Why can I only view the P&L?** — Editing needs permission to manage the business’s items. Ask the account owner.

**I typed “Zomato commission” and it asked why.** — Commission and other platform figures are already counted automatically. Adding them again would count them twice, so Mynt asks for a reason first.

**Where are TDS, TCS and loan repayments?** — Under <b>Not part of profit</b>. They move cash but are not profit or loss.
